How it works

Geofencing meets behavioral finance.

01
Detect your location
CoreLocation geofences trigger silently when you arrive at a store — no manual input, no battery drain.
02
Rank your cards
TapSmart's recommendation engine scores every card in your wallet against the merchant category and finds your best return.
03
Notify before you tap
A lock screen notification fires instantly so you know which card to pull up in Apple Pay before you're at the register.

About

Builder, researcher, founder.

I'm Prisha Bhanushali, a student at West Valley College (dual-enrolled at Westmont High School) and a SMASH UC Berkeley Scholar with two years of independent ML research in agricultural computer vision.

I started TapSmart because I kept leaving cashback on the table — not from laziness, but because knowing the optimal card in the moment is genuinely hard. I built the entire iOS app from scratch: CoreLocation geofencing, SwiftUI, UserNotifications, and Apple Pay integration via PassKit.

I've deferred my Chancellor's Scholarship at UMass Amherst to focus on TapSmart full-time.
