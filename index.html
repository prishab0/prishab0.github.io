FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does TapSmart need my credit-card numbers?+
No. You simply select which cards you own — never any card numbers.
Do I need to connect my bank?+
No bank connection is required to use the current beta.
How does TapSmart know which card is best?+
TapSmart compares the reward structures of the cards you've selected with the merchant or category you're visiting, and recommends the best available reward.
What about rotating 5% categories?+
TapSmart can account for whether an eligible rotating bonus is activated and whether you've reached the applicable spending cap — so it recommends the rate that's actually available to you.
Why isn't my card available?+
We're actively expanding card coverage during the beta. If yours isn't available yet, tell us which card you're looking for and we'll use requests to prioritize what we add next. Request a card →
Is TapSmart free?+
Yes — TapSmart is completely free during the beta.
Is TapSmart available on Android?+
Not yet. TapSmart is currently iPhone-only while it's in beta.
